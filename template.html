Welcome

This guide wraps an archive kept by Gregg's assistant. It collects the class WhatsApp group and Masonic email in one place, so nothing gets lost: events (even ones mentioned in passing), flyers and photos, Masonic videos and useful links.

Everything links to a shared Google Drive folder: Harmony #9 Archive (Images, Videos). Files open in view-only mode.

This guide contains no ritual or study material and never will. Dates come from chat messages, flyers and email. Always confirm with the lodge or the Trestle Board before you travel.
