Welcome

This guide wraps an archive kept by Gregg's assistant. It collects the class WhatsApp group and Masonic email in one place, so nothing gets lost: the official Master Mason Rookie Award checklist, events (even ones mentioned in passing), flyers and photos, Masonic videos and useful links.

Photos, flyers and videos play right on this page, including on an iPhone. Documents still open in the shared Google Drive archive.

This guide contains no ritual or study material and never will. Dates come from chat messages, flyers and email. Always confirm with the lodge or the Trestle Board before you travel.
